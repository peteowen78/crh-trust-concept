What do the fees include?

Fees are paid monthly and include your room, full board, laundry of personal items and 24 hour care. Hairdressing, chiropody, opticians and physiotherapy are extra, as are newspapers, dry cleaning and a private phone line. Call us and we will talk you through the costs.

Can we visit whenever we like?

Yes. Visit as often as you like. Residents are free to come and go with relatives and friends; just let the Manager know.

Will someone go with Mum to appointments?

As far as possible, nobody ever goes to a medical, dental or eye appointment alone.

Is there help paying for a short stay?

Help with funding is available in many cases for respite and day care. We can tell you what might apply.

Can we try it before deciding?

Yes. Many people start with a respite stay, and when our guest room is free you can stay for a short time to see how easy it is to settle in.

Who inspects the home?

Our homes are regularly inspected by the Care Quality Commission, the independent regulator. You can read the latest reports on our Quality page.

Three homes, one trust. Find the right one.

All three are run by the Cheshire Residential Homes Trust, a charity since 1947. Every one offers residential care, respite breaks and day care.

The new Trepassey was made possible by a gift in a will.

In 2019 Trepassey was extended and refurbished to a very high standard, thanks to a multi-million pound legacy.

The trust itself began the same way: with people giving what they could. In 1947 sixteen governors, all but three of them women, raised £837 6s 6d to found homes for older people in need of help.
