A Legacy of Care, since 1947

Sixteen governors founded this trust. Thirteen of them were women.

In 1947 a Cheshire County Councillor called a meeting to improve care for older people, and a charity was formed to fund homes for those in need of assistance. By April that year, £837 6s 6d had been raised.

All but three of the original sixteen Governors were women, and their determined efforts set the trust on its way. Nearly eighty years on, the same charity runs three homes in Cheshire and on the Wirral.

“A Legacy of Care is one of the best ways you can continue to contribute to the future lives of others.”
