A good day at Upton Grange

Join in with everything, or nothing at all. These are the things residents enjoy most, in their own time.

Morning

Breakfast your way

In your own room if you prefer, then morning coffee with friends in the lounge when you are ready.

Late morning

Into the garden

Walks round the gardens, a seat in the sunny courtyard, or a little light weeding.

Afternoon

Art, music and visitors

Art afternoons in the Orangery, choirs and entertainers, pet therapists, and performances by local schoolchildren.

Evening

Tea, then a film

Afternoon tea with homemade cakes, a light supper, then a film together in the lounge or TV in your room.

A house, a garden and a kitchen, not a ward

Once a private country house, Upton Grange is still a home first. Rooms have their own bathrooms and room for your own armchair and bits and bobs. Our chef cooks varied menus from good local food, tempting even when appetites are small.

  • Two lounges, one of them quiet, with a small library
  • The Orangery, bright and perfect for visitors
  • A central courtyard that is a sun trap all year round
  • Hairdressing salon, and regular visits from the GP

Living here, staying a while, or coming for the day

Every guest has the same care, meals and company as our residents. Help with funding is available in many cases for short stays.

Make it home

Residential care with 24 hour support, your own en suite room, with board, laundry and care included in one monthly fee.
